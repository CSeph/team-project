## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* Which platform will you use for communication outside of class, when required for work on your course project? (Examples: Text message, Discord, WeChat, etc.)

- Group communication platforms are created on Whatsapp and Discord.

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages in at most how long? 1 day? 2 days? Some other amount of time?

- Within 2 days (48 hours); the sooner the better.

* What things should a teammate notify you about? (Examples: if they think they won't be able to meet a deadline, if they have to miss lecture, etc.)

- If one expects they will miss a team meeting, or be late to tutorials, lectures, meetings, etc.
- Teammates can communicate about distribution of responsibilities further down the line as well
- Teammates should communicate challenges faced during completion of project throughout the process

* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication. What are some respectful and inclusive behaviours you expect when communicating with each other during lectures, labs, or outside of class? (Examples: Actively listening to each team members ideas, giving everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute, etc.)

- Refrain from aggressive behaviour
- Communication should proceed after a full description of the problem at hand
- Actively listening to each team members ideas
- Giving everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute

## Decision Making

* How will decisions about your team project be made? (Examples: By majority vote, by unanimous vote, etc.)

- Explicit majority vote on decisions about project

## Conflict resolution

* How will your team resolve conflicts? (Example: by listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise. By consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.)

- If conflict is persistent, the TA/Prof will be consulted
- Conflict in decisions should be taken to the group for majority vote

## Accountability

* Reliability and accountability are also important aspects of teamwork. What are the responsibilities of each team member? (Example: completing their share of the work in a timely manner, seeking assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors when required, etc.)

-  At least two people should review the pull request before approving
- Prior to pushing changes, change-maker should inform rest of group before pushing, describing exact details of the change
- Notify about pull requests (PRs) and put in detailed code descriptions in such requests
